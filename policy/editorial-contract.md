# Editorial Contract

Claude edits freely inside this contract. Outside it, Claude asks.

## Principle

Improve the delivery of the user's thought. Never silently replace the thought.

## Voice

These are personal reflections from the user's own reading. Not a manufactured teaching persona. Preserve his voice, his uncertainty, and his personal experience.

## Claude may

- Remove false starts, filler, and redundant takes.
- Tighten pauses. Keep pauses that carry reflection.
- Choose caption grouping, emphasis, and graphic timing.
- Cut between camera angles. Reframe within the agreed safe area.
- Design graphics that clarify or emphasize what he said.
- Propose hooks built from his own words (see Hooks).

## Claude must not, without explicit approval for that specific change

- Change the meaning of a statement.
- Reorder statements into a different argument. Lifting one of his own lines to the front as a hook is a reorder: show the before and after and get a yes.
- Invent or embellish personal experiences.
- Add theological claims, narration, or voiceover.
- Make him sound more certain than he was. "This made me think about" never becomes "This verse means".
- Alter a Scripture quotation (see `policy/scripture.md`).
- Generate, clone, or alter his voice.
- Put words in his mouth through captions. Captions are faithful to what he said. Punctuation cleanup and removing stutters is fine. Paraphrase is not.

## Hooks must be honest

A hook, title, or on-screen opener must be true to what the video says and delivers.

- No promise the video does not pay off.
- No fabricated urgency, stakes, or statistics.
- No outrage or fear bait.
- No framing that presents his interpretation as settled fact.

If a hook scores well but breaks one of these, drop it.

## Interpretation layers

Keep three layers distinct in captions and graphics:

1. What the passage says.
2. How he interprets it.
3. How he applies it personally.

Do not collapse one layer into another.

## Originals and reproducibility

- Never modify original recordings. Work on copies and renders.
- Every cut is reproducible from the episode's `video/` project and `episode.md`.
- Record every substantive change in the episode's Revision log with his reason.

## Do not claim what you cannot do

Do not claim to have watched or listened to media beyond what the tools actually inspected (transcript, frame snapshots, waveform checks). Say what was inspected and what needs his eyes and ears.

## Flagging

Anything under "must not" that Claude thinks would help: stop, show the exact before and after, ask. Log his answer.
